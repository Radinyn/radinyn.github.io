About me

I am Igor Urbanik.

Currently:

  • PhD Candidate at the Jagiellonian Center for Artificial Intelligence in Kraków, Poland, supervised by Jacek Tabor and Tomasz Danel.
  • Data Scientist (part-time) at Unit8, solving various problems with data and AI.

In the past:

  • MEng in Data Science at the AGH University of Krakow, with my Master's thesis written at the Embodied Neural Intelligence Lab under the supervision of Paweł Gajewski.
  • BEng in Computer Science at the AGH University of Krakow.
  • Member of AGH Space Systems, where I developed autonomous navigation for the Kalman Rover, successful at multiple international competitions from the Rover Challenge series.
  • Software Engineer at Software Mansion, where I worked on Starknet Foundry - a Starknet smart contract development toolchain.
